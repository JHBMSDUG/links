## About JHBMSDUG

JHBMSDUG is the Johannesburg Microsoft Developer User Group. We are a community of Microsoft technology enthusiasts, students and professionals who come together to share knowledge, network, and learn about the latest developments in Microsoft related products and technologies.

We meet the 2nd Tuesday of every month at the Microsoft Office in Sandton, Johannesburg. Visit our [Meetup page](https://www.meetup.com/JHBMSDUG/) to RSVP and join our group.
